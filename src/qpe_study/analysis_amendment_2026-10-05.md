## Metrics and preservation

Per design and method: point-rule truth inclusion, numerical-union inclusion, median/mean union length, IQR, full-branch/empty/disconnected frequencies, component counts, elapsed seconds and cell counts. Exact Clopper–Pearson MC intervals use Bonferroni across method-by-design proportions within each separately declared family. They are not joint across families. Paired ratio-of-median width percentiles use 5000 within-design resamples, seed 2026100504; descriptive and pointwise only.

All outcomes, including empty sets, noncovering results and failed runs, are retained. Inference errors stop the relevant run; partial results cannot be promoted to completed evidence. Resume only against exact protocol/source/input hashes. Scientific corrections are recorded separately from the original protocol. Earlier numerical implementations are retained as shared dependencies.
